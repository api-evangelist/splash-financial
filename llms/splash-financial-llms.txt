# Splash Financial

> Splash Financial is a financial technology company and loan marketplace that helps consumers compare personal loans, student loan refinancing, and home equity options from lender partners. Splash Financial is not a direct lender. The company's stated mission is to make people more powerful than their debt.

Last reviewed: 2026-05-11. Rates, eligibility, loan terms, lender availability, approval, funding timing, and disclaimers may change. Use the linked product pages and disclaimers for current details.
